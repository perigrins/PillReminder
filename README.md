## Pill Reminder

A birth control pill reminder app that helps users keep track of their daily pills.
Users can choose their preferred pill schedule, such as 21+7 or 24+4, and track each pill they take.

The app sends notifications to remind users when it's time to take their pill.
Users can also create an account and log in to keep their data securely stored in Firebase, which is used for both authentication and the database.
